Shrimp Tempura Recipe

All you really need for this recipe are a few ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 cup all purpose flour - sifted
  • 2/3 cup of very cold or chilled water
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch

Steps:

  1. Clean all the shrimps, and pat dry them.
  2. Heat oil in medium heat while you prepare the shrimps.
  3. Mix the ingredients together. Don't overmix, clumps are good!
  4. On a separate plate, mix some cornstarch and flour together.
  5. Dip the shrimps in the batter and then the dry flour and cornstarch mixture.
  6. Fry the shrimps until golden brown.
  7. Drain excess oil and serve with your favorite dipping sauce!
